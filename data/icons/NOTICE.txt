dev.hyprshell.Settings.svg and dev.hyprshell.Settings-symbolic.svg are the
GNOME Settings (gnome-control-center) application icons, renamed so the
hypr-shell-settings desktop entry can use them:

  /usr/share/icons/hicolor/scalable/apps/org.gnome.Settings.svg
  /usr/share/icons/hicolor/symbolic/apps/org.gnome.Settings-symbolic.svg
  (gnome-control-center 50.4, Arch Linux package)

They are the work of the GNOME design team and are distributed under the
GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, like gnome-control-center
itself (https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/gnome-control-center). They are
bundled here for the user's own installation; keep this notice with them.
